Everything you need

Simple to start. Satisfying to keep.

All the essentials of a money tracker, made quick to use and pleasant to come back to.

📖

A real passbook

Flip through your entries as bank-passbook pages: drag a page to turn it, read one up close, or go full screen in landscape. Black-and-white print included.

⚡

Two-tap entry

A built-in calculator keypad (120 + 45 × 2), the category you usually pick at this hour already chosen, and your past notes one tap away.

🎯

Budgets & goals

Daily, weekly or monthly budgets with a clear status, and savings goals that show how much to put aside each month.

📊

Insights

Donut, ranking, trend, a spending calendar and cash flow, with a comparison to the previous period and a monthly recap to share.

🔁

Recurring bills

Rent, salary and subscriptions that repeat weekly, monthly or yearly. Confirm or skip each one with a tap.

🎨

Make it yours

Six themes plus your wallpaper's colours, each with its own app icon, light and dark modes, a home-screen widget and app lock.

Private by design

Your money stays on your phone.

Penny Pages has no servers and no accounts. The app does not even have permission to use the internet, so your entries cannot be sent anywhere.
