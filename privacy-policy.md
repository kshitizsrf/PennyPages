# Penny Pages privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Penny Pages ("the app") is an offline expense and budget tracker published on Google Play by Bits4Bytes ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Information the app handles

You may enter the following into the app:

- Transactions: amounts, categories, dates, times and notes
- Categories, budgets and preferences such as your currency, theme and language
- Recurring payments, savings goals and the name you choose to be greeted by

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app has no servers, no user accounts and no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and it does not collect or share personal or device information.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup, such as your Google account backup. That backup is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by the app.

## App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never sees or stores your biometric data or your PIN, pattern or password.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. The app also checks each morning, on the device, whether a recurring payment you set up is due and tells you with a local notification. No data leaves the device.

## Home-screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows today's spending and what is left of your budget on your home screen. While app lock is on, the widget shows no figures.

## Device settings the app reads

To save battery and respect your accessibility choices, the app checks whether battery saver is on and whether animations are turned off on your device, and calms its animations if so. It only reads these settings on the device; it does not store or send them.

## Sharing your monthly recap

When you choose to share a monthly recap, the app creates an image in its private cache and opens Android's share sheet. The image is sent only to the app or person you pick.

## Exporting your data

When you export to CSV, the file is saved to the location you choose through the system file picker. After that, you decide what happens to the file.

## Importing data

When you import a CSV file, the app reads only the file you pick through the system file picker and adds its entries to the app's storage on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

## Sample data

If you choose to try sample data on first launch, the app creates example entries on your device. They can be removed in one tap and contain no information about you.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions and categories in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
